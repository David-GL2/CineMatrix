Why CineMatrix when Plex or Jellyfin already exist? Those are server-based media managers, great if you want to watch your collection from your phone or the living room TV, but they require setup, configuration and upkeep. CineMatrix is the opposite: a desktop app you open, point at your folder, and you're done. Built for people who want their collection organized on their own PC, without standing up infrastructure to get there.You only need to connect to the internet when you scan.

Features

Everything you need, nothing you don't

Every feature solves something you'll genuinely run into when cataloging a large, messy collection.

SCANNING

Automatically identify your files

Reads the filename and fills in title, year, synopsis, genre, country, cover art and with the technical data of the file. Works the same for movies and TV shows.

NOTHING LOST

Nothing gets left out

If a file can't be identified automatically, it's kept as "pending" instead of dropped. Resolve it later with a manual search, whenever you want.

MULTI-LIBRARY

Several drives, several libraries

Keep one named library per hard drive and switch between them — handy if your content lives in different places and isn't always plugged in.

ORGANIZATION

Categories your way

On top of genre, country and continent, create your own themed categories — from "Kung Fu" to whatever you come up with — and combine them freely.

COLLECTIONS

Franchises, grouped on their own

If you have several movies from the same franchise, they show up grouped as their own entry — open it and see them all together, no hunting one by one.

PLAYBACK

With the player you already use

One click opens the file with your default Windows player. CineMatrix doesn't play anything itself — it only organizes and launches.

TV SHOWS

Seasons and episodes, sorted

Detects that a group of files belongs to the same show and organizes them by season — each episode with its own real title and synopsis, not just the filename.

PRIVACY

No accounts, no cloud

No sign-up, no login, no data of yours ever leaves your machine. Everything you scan stays on your own drive. You just need to connect at the moment you identify.

A look inside

Here's what it looks like

Five screens — just enough to get a feel for how it's used day to day.

Library

Everything in one grid, at a glance

Cover art, favorite and watched status right on each card. No text lists, no opening every entry just to know what's what.

  • 9 tokens per line and self-regulated size
  • Badges for favorite, watched and "pending identification"
Filters

Find what you're after in seconds

Category, genre, continent, country and your own tags, all on a single line. Favorites and watched are separate toggle buttons, so you never lose a click.

  • Combine several filters at once
  • Animation as its own category, not mixed into genre
TV Shows

Seasons and episodes, already grouped

CineMatrix recognizes that a group of files belongs to the same show and organizes them by season — each episode with its own title and synopsis, not just the filename.

  • Detects multiple versions of the same episode without duplicating the season
  • Works just as well with ongoing shows as with finished ones
Movie page

All the info, without hunting elsewhere for it

Synopsis, technical file details (resolution, audio, subtitles) and quick actions to play, mark as favorite, or fix the identification if it got something wrong.

  • Manual correction if the scan got it wrong
  • Direct link to the full collection, if it belongs to one
Collections

Franchises, grouped as their own entry

Pick "Collections" in the category filter and instead of a list of names, you get one card per franchise — just like a show groups its episodes. Open one and every movie in it is right there.

  • Movies only: TV shows never mix in here
Privacy

Your data stays on your machine. Full stop.

CineMatrix has no server and no user account of its own — there's nowhere to upload your data even if it wanted to.

  • Everything is saved on your PC — your library, categories and preferences, in a local folder next to the executable.
  • No accounts, no sign-up. Open it and use it — nothing to create, nothing to log into.
  • No ads, no tracking. No behavioral analytics, no advertising SDKs.
  • Only two outside connections: TheTVDB (to look up titles and cover art) and Microsoft Store (to verify your license).
