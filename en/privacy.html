Privacy policy

Last updated: August 2026

CineMatrix is a Windows desktop application that catalogs and organizes video files you already have on your own computer. This page explains, plainly, what data the app handles, where it comes from, and what's never collected.

Quick summary

  • All your data (your library, categories, preferences) is saved on your own PC, next to the app's executable.
  • CineMatrix's developer never receives, stores, or can see any of that data.
  • There are no user accounts, no sign-up, no login.
  • There are no ads and no tracking for advertising purposes.
  • The app makes network requests to two outside services, described below: TheTVDB (metadata and cover art) and Microsoft Store (license verification).

What gets saved, and where

When you scan a folder, CineMatrix creates a data folder next to the application's own executable. That's where the following is saved, in local text files:

  • Each movie or show's entry (title, year, genres, synopsis, the file's path on your drive, etc.).
  • Downloaded cover art, saved as images in that same folder.
  • Your custom categories, favorites and "watched" marks.
  • The name and last scanned path of each library you've created.

None of this leaves your computer except in the two specific cases explained below. If you uninstall the app or delete that data folder, that information is gone for good — there's no copy anywhere else, because there never was one.

Communication with outside services

TheTVDB (metadata and cover art)

When you scan a folder or correct a title's identification, CineMatrix sends the search text (the filename, already processed) directly from your machine to the TheTVDB API, to get the title, synopsis, genre, country and cover art. This request doesn't include any personal data of yours — no name, no location, no user identifier, because the app never asks for or knows any of that. How TheTVDB handles those requests on their end (technical server logs, for instance) is governed by their own policy, which you can check at thetvdb.com.

Microsoft Store (license)

If you installed CineMatrix from Microsoft Store, the app asks Windows, on startup, whether your installation has a valid license (the purchase you made in the Store). This check is handled by the operating system itself; CineMatrix never sees or stores any payment data — that's processed directly by Microsoft, under their own privacy statement.

What CineMatrix never does

  • No ads and no advertising SDKs.
  • No usage analytics and no behavioral tracking.
  • Never uploads your library, your files, or their names to any server owned by the developer — because no such server exists.
  • Never accesses any folder on your drive beyond the ones you explicitly choose to scan.
  • Doesn't play or stream any audiovisual content itself: it simply opens your files with whichever player you already have set as default on Windows.

Children's privacy

CineMatrix is not directed at children and doesn't knowingly collect data from minors; in fact, it doesn't collect personal data from any user at all, as explained above.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in any meaningful way, the date above will be updated and it will be noted in the project's repository when the change is significant.
